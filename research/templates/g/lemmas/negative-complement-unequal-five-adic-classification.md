Assume
\[
L_+,\qquad Q_3<0,\qquad D=ge,\qquad v_5(g)<v_5(D),
\]
and
\[
5^F\mid(q-1)e-4Qg,
\qquad q=2^{2a}Q-1,\qquad q\equiv\pm2\pmod5.
\]
Then
\[
v_5(q-1)=v_5(Q)=0.
\]
Put
\[
\gamma=v_5(g),\qquad \varepsilon=v_5(e)>0.
\]

The lift is equivalent to exactly one of the following residual alternatives:

1. Large valuations:
   \[
   \min(\varepsilon,\gamma)\ge F.
   \]

2. Equal valuations below \(F\):
   \[
   \varepsilon=\gamma=h,\qquad 1\le h<F,
   \]
   and, writing \(e=5^he_0\), \(g=5^hg_0\),
   \[
   (q-1)e_0\equiv4Qg_0\pmod{5^{F-h}}.
   \]

If \(\varepsilon\ne\gamma\), divisibility holds exactly when
\[
\min(\varepsilon,\gamma)\ge F.
\]
If \(\varepsilon=\gamma\ge F\), the lift is automatic.

The exact-zero possibility
\[
(q-1)e-4Qg=0
\]
is impossible because
\[
v_2((q-1)e)=1,\qquad v_2(4Qg)\ge2.
\]
